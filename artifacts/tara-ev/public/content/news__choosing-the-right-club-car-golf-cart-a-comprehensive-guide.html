How Long Do Utility Task Vehicle Batteries Last?

One of the most crucial components in an electric utility task vehicle is the battery. Typically, high-quality utility task vehicle batteries last between 4 to 6 years, depending on usage, maintenance, and climate. For instance, lithium-ion batteries—featured in many TARA Utility Task Vehicles (UTV)—often last longer than traditional lead-acid options and require less maintenance.

Key tips to extend battery life:

  • Regularly charge your cart after each use.
  • Avoid deep discharges whenever possible.
  • Store your cart in a covered, temperature-controlled environment.
